Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

ClipBird ("the app") is a personal software project. This page describes, for development and testing purposes, how the app handles data.

What data is processed

ClipBird processes video files and links that a user provides, in order to transcribe them and generate short clips. Account information (name, email) is stored to allow the user to log in and manage their own projects.

Third-party services

To function, ClipBird may send audio/video data to third-party AI services for transcription and analysis, and may connect to third-party platforms (such as YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, or Facebook) on the user's request, solely to publish clips to accounts the user has explicitly connected and authorized.

Data retention

Uploaded videos, generated clips, and account data are stored only for as long as needed to provide the service to the user, and are not sold or shared with third parties beyond what is required to operate the service as described above.

Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, contact the developer directly.
